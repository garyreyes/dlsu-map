## Design specificity

Chrome is deliberately the familiar map-app convention (right for Operate). Product character lives in the copy
(room codes, "Loading the campus…", O1). Visually S1 is generic until 2c's massing model lands. Detector: 0 findings
in source, built HTML and in-page scans of 4 views. The live overlay can't run under the app's CSP
(`script-src 'self'`); in-page scan ran via evaluate. First-pass in-page hits (gradient-text, marquee,
flat-type-hierarchy) were artefacts of the injection, verified false.

## Priority issues

- [P1] Losing signal mid-session wipes the app to O1 (status.ts offlineView 'saving'+offline; App.tsx early return).
  Fix: O1 only when the app can't boot; after boot, keep the app and show "Saving paused…" in the O2 slot.
- [P1] Failed or impossible save is silent ('not-saved' online, 'unsupported'). Fix: persistent O2-slot plate.
- [P1] Map failed is indistinguishable from loading and is a dead end. Fix: split by cause (chunk vs WebGL), icon +
  headline + one line, Reload button only where reload helps, say search still works.
- [P2] White first paint then hard cut to near-black; no theme-color. Fix in 2c with D13.
- [P2] Duplicate search entry (HomeHint mirrors the pill) and "Beta" twice (NotOfficialNote).
